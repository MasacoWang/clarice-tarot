About Clarice's Tarot

A space created for reflection, not prediction. Tarot is a mirror — it shows you what already lives within.

🌱

Rooted in Intention

Each reading begins with a breath. The cards don't tell the future — they illuminate the patterns, hopes, and wisdom you carry right now.

🕊️

Gentle & Affirming

No fear, no doom. Whether you draw the Tower or the Star, every card is an invitation to grow. Our interpretations focus on healing and self-compassion.

📖

Two Great Traditions

Choose between the intuitive Rider-Waite tradition and the esoteric depth of the Thoth deck. Both paths lead to clarity.

🌿

Designed with Care

A calm, beautiful space to slow down. No ads, no data collection, no noise. Just you and the cards, in a moment of stillness.

💼

Career & Purpose

Feeling stuck at work? Considering a change? Tarot can help you explore your relationship with ambition, fulfillment, and professional growth.

Family & Relationships
